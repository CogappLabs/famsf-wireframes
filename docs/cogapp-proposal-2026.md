# Executive summary {#executive-summary}

Thank you for inviting us to send you this proposal for your digital collections project. It is the latest in a series of ambitious projects between Fine Arts Museums of San Francisco (FAMSF) and Cogapp, and we are delighted to partner with you to deliver a world-class platform. 

You are looking to modernize the management and display of FAMSF’s collections. You wish to move away from your current online collection architecture which is clunky and problematic. 

We will partner with you to support and contribute to a rigorous discovery process to agree a project vision, understand your audiences and priorities, and to collaborate and ideate towards the optimal solution and set of features for a launchable new collection in this, Phase 1\. Discovery will result in a prioritized backlog of infrastructure and features for this project, and a roadmap of inspirational ideas for the long-term future, Phase 2\.

The new solution should work in harmony with your existing systems (e.g. TMS), be robust, engaging and forward thinking, and be both responsive to your online visitors’ devices and any accessibility needs they may have. 

It should build a foundation for potential expansion of the collections, both for enhancements for the presentation of your objects and data, but also potentially as a wider Bay Area collections aggregator. 

At launch, you will have an automated collections data and asset pipeline system that displays your rich assets, metadata and interpretation beautifully in an intuitive public interface designed for your online visitors, existing and new. Audiences will be able to search, browse, and explore FAMSF's holdings in great detail, without changing the way the Museums manage collections data and assets. This will be architected, designed and delivered with Phase 2 enhancements in mind. 

The rest of this document responds to your comprehensive brief, describes how we will help to define and then meet your requirements in Discovery, and outlines how we will work collaboratively and iteratively to deliver a world-class online expression of the FAMSF collection. It also includes our method to deliver, and costs. We have included appendices of case studies and team biographies.

Cogapp is the leading digital agency in the world when it comes to presenting artworks online. We have vast experience of digital design, development and delivery for museums, galleries, libraries, and archives. Online collections are our speciality. 

We estimate a cost of **$350,000** to deliver Phase 1 of an exemplary online collection. 

# Project plan {#project-plan}

As detailed in your brief, this project will be split into Discovery and Implementation. During both strands of work we will collaborate to ensure that we consider not just the immediate aims of Phase 1, delivering your near-term “Must haves”, but also lay the groundwork and take steps towards an inspirational Phase 2 roadmap to drive extra funding that will move FAMSF into a leadership position amongst peer organizations related to how collections are presented and engaged with online.

## Discovery {#discovery}

For this project, we will collaborate with the FAMSF team whose expertise and availability may allow Discovery activities to commence before the Cogapp team join the project in earnest. In practice, we expect many of the Discovery activities outlined here to be addressed in house by FAMSF. 

When Cogapp’s Discovery phase begins, it will be time-boxed and we will ensure all necessary research and investigation has taken place to de-risk the project, understand the landscape and build a prioritized backlog of work grounded in the higher-level strategic aims of the organization.

We generally divide this phase into several concurrent, interlinked workstreams that include strategy, user experience (UX), design, and technical discovery. Roughly these can be split into areas where we explore the human elements with technical discovery focusing on data and systems. In this case, some of these areas will be covered in more detail by FAMSF, and some by Cogapp, while collaborating throughout.

This approach will suit the needs of FAMSF well in terms of exploring the long-term aims for the collection online whilst also identifying areas where immediate action will result in a much-improved experience for your audiences and internal team.

These workstreams focus on the "human" element—getting under the skin of your organization, understanding your audience, and defining the visual and strategic direction of the project. We structure this using our three-stage design process: Immersion, Direction, and Iteration.

### Implementation phase activities {#implementation-phase-activities}

At Cogapp, our technical implementation is driven by a commitment to robust engineering, future-proof standards, and creating a "prosperous working environment" for both our developers and your team. We don't just build websites; we build sustainable digital ecosystems.

Our technical implementation usually follows an Agile methodology, broken down into focused sprints. Here are the core activities you can expect during this phase:

#### 1\. Infrastructure and environment setup

We lay a solid foundation before writing feature code to ensure stability and security from day one.

Environment Configuration: We typically establish three distinct environments: Development (for internal building), Staging (for your review and testing), and Production (the live site).

Automated Deployment: We set up automated deployment scripts and continuous integration (CI) pipelines. This ensures that code moves safely between environments, reducing human error and allowing for frequent, reliable updates.

Performance & Security Layering: We configure Content Delivery Networks (CDNs) like Cloudflare early on to handle caching, enhance speed globally, and provide protection against DDOS attacks.

#### 2\. Collections and data integration

We will build sophisticated pipelines to surface your data without disrupting your internal systems of record.

Data Harvesting (The Mill): We build custom "Harvesters" to extract metadata from your internal systems (e.g. TMS). We then use our "Mill" software to validate, clean, and transform this data into a format suitable for the web.

Search Indexing: We implement high-performance search engines like Elasticsearch. We configure these to handle complex queries, faceted filtering, and features like "did you mean?" or autocomplete, ensuring users can find exactly what they need instantly.

API Development: We will build a custom API to serve this processed data to the front end, ensuring a clean separation between your data and the user interface.

These API endpoints will also be available to your existing CMS, enabling your editors to treat the online collection as a resource to call upon for content programs.

#### 3\. Image and media pipelines

We ensure your visual assets are delivered in the highest quality possible.

IIIF Implementation: We are experts in the International Image Interoperability Framework (IIIF). We set up pipelines to automatically convert your master images (e.g., TIFFs) into deep-zoom compatible formats (like Pyramidal TIFFs) served via high-performance image servers like Cantaloupe.

#### 4\. Frontend engineering

This is where we bring the design to life for the user. 

Component Development: We build the user interface using modern frameworks. We work in a modular way, creating a library of reusable components that ensure consistency across the site.

Responsive & Accessible Implementation: We code mobile-first, ensuring the site works beautifully on all devices. Crucially, we build with accessibility in mind from the start, ensuring keyboard navigation, screen reader compatibility, and adherence to WCAG 2.2 AA standards.

#### 5\. Quality assurance (QA) and hardening

We believe quality is everyone's responsibility, but we also have dedicated processes to verify it.

Peer Code Review: No code reaches the main codebase without being reviewed by another developer. This ensures best practices are followed and knowledge is shared across the team.

Automated Testing: We use linters and automated test suites to check code quality and functionality programmatically.

Hardening Sprint: Before launch, we dedicate specific time to a "hardening" phase. This includes load testing (simulating high traffic), security scanning, and a final thorough accessibility audit.

By rigorously following these steps, we ensure that the digital products we build are not just visually stunning, but also technically sound, secure, and built to last.

### Collections publication process {#collections-publication-process}

Given the importance of how your collections data is published, the approach proposed in the [Technical Approach](#technical-approach-summary) section is expanded in more detail below.

#### Collections data pipeline

At Cogapp, our approach to data harvesting is built on decades of experience extracting value from diverse archival and collection management systems. We have developed a robust, adaptable software architecture designed to securely extract data from your internal systems, validate it, and transform it for public consumption without disrupting your core operations.

Our methodology ensures that your website remains fast, reliable, and insulated from any performance issues within your internal network. Here are the specific technical steps we typically undertake to harvest your data:

##### 1\. Configuration of Harvesters

For each of your data sources, whether it be your collection management system TMS, or an asset repository for images and so forth, we configure a specific "Harvester."

Extraction: The harvester connects to your source system (via API, SQL database connection, or file export) to extract raw metadata and media references.

Scheduled operation: This process typically runs unattended on a nightly basis to ensure the website is always up to date with the latest work from your catalogers and curators.

##### 2\. Validation and The Mill

Once the data is harvested, it enters "The Mill." This is our central processing engine that creates a contract between your source data and the public website.

Schema Validation: We test the incoming data against an agreed schema (e.g., XSD for XML or JSON Schema). This ensures that if the structure of your internal data changes unexpectedly, the harvest pauses rather than publishing invalid data to the public website.

Sanity Checks: We apply logical checks—such as ensuring a minimum number of artworks are present—to prevent partial data loads from ever going live.

##### 3\. Transformation and Mapping

We do not simply copy data; we refine it. We use standard mapping scripts (typically XSLT or JSLT) to transform your internal data structure into a format optimized for the web.

Data Cleaning: We standardize terminology, fix formatting (such as dates), and merge fields where necessary.

Business Logic: We apply your specific rules for publication, such as filtering out objects not marked for public view, formatting attribution lines correctly, or combining artist names in a specific way.

##### 4\. Indexing and Storage

The transformed data is then uploaded to a high-performance search engine, typically Elasticsearch.

Search Optimization: This allows us to power advanced features like faceted search, "did you mean?" suggestions, and tunable relevance rankings.

Separation of Concerns: By pushing data to an intermediate search index, we decouple the public website from your internal systems. If your internal database goes offline for maintenance, your website continues to function perfectly using the indexed data.

##### 5\. Blue/Green Deployment

To ensure zero downtime, we utilize a "Blue/Green" deployment strategy.

Seamless Switching: We build the new search index in the background. Only once it is fully built and verified do we instantaneously switch the website to point to the new index. This ensures users never experience a broken or empty search page.

##### 6\. Reporting

Transparency is key to our process. Upon completion of the harvest, the system automatically emails a summary report to your team.

Detailed Insights: This report details the number of records added, removed, or changed, and highlights any warnings or errors (such as missing images or incomplete citations), helping your team identify data quality issues in the source system.

#### Parallel Image Pipelines

Alongside metadata, we often deploy parallel pipelines for visual assets. We will use our mature tooling to automatically convert master images (e.g., TIFFs) into web-ready IIIF formats.

During discovery we will explore the possibilities for FAMSF’s long-term vision for the online collection, we will then narrow into shorter-term delivery goals with the intention of delivering a much improved experience for audiences engaging with FAMSF’s artworks online.
