Planning Doc

Collection Project 2026

Website Survey

# Context

This survey is part of the Discovery phase of FAMSF's 2026 Collections Project - a strategic initiative to modernize how the museum's digital collection is managed, maintained, and experienced by the public. As part of the discovery process, we will post an online survey to collections specific areas of the website to gather visitor feedback on the current experience.

# Methodology

* **Format:** Online digital survey
* **Location:** [famsf.org/collections](http://famsf.org/collections) + [famsf.org/art-finder](https://www.famsf.org/art-finder)
* **Audience:** General audience, Researchers
* **Dates:** April 13 - Mary 13, 2026
* **Draft deadline:** April 8, 2026

**Key learning goals for survey:**

* What problems do visitors have with the current collections website?
* What functionality or features matter most to visitors?
* What problems or frustrations exist today that we have an opportunity to solve?
* What does the current website do well?

**Output:** This synthesis report and list of strategic opportunities for the 2026 Collections project.
