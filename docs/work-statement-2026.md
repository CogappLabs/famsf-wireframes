## 1\. Project 

Discovery and implementation of a new Online Collection platform for the Fine Arts Museums of San Francisco (“FAMSF”), replacing the current collections architecture with a modern, performant, automated, and scalable solution integrated with TMS and [famsf.org](http://famsf.org).

## 3\. Site 

The work will be carried out online, at the Fine Arts Museums of San Francisco and at Cogapp’s offices in Brighton, UK. 

## 4\. Services 

Our services will be determined in collaboration with Fine Arts Museums of San Francisco and will include:

**Discovery**

* Stakeholder interview review  
  * To be conducted by FAMSF and a summary made available to Cogapp  
* Audience analysis and user research   
  * Cogapp to review existing material  
* Landscape research  
  * To be conducted by FAMSF. landscape research will include both in and out of category examples of complex, online collections of academic and/or research-oriented materials. Research will include a summary of standard features and functionality, as well as exceptional and/or desirable features and functionality. Cogapp may conduct supplemental landscape research, however this activity shall not exceed twenty hours of effort.  
* Current experience audit  
  * To be conducted by FAMSF. The current experience audit shall include an evaluation of existing features, functionality, content, and site performance.  CogApp may conduct supplemental experience auditing, however this activity shall not exceed twenty hours of effort.  
* Definition of business goals  
  * To be conducted by FAMSF. Business goals shall be delivered during CogApp onboarding and shall include high-level measures of success (to be refined upon final feature set selection at the end of Discovery).   
* Feature ideation and prioritization  
* Technical discovery and systems architecture design  
  * FAMSF responsibilities (pre-project, to be completed before Cogapp engagement begins):  
    * Provide read-only credentials and access to the TMS database, ideally, only tables that FAMSF are happy for Cogapp to view  
    * Any documentation required for access  
    * Definition of what other data FAMSF want to pull through to the collection website that is not currently surfaced  
  * Cogapp responsibilities (during discovery):  
    * Cogapp will conduct a technical discovery and produce a systems architecture design based on materials provided by FAMSF. This will include:  
      * Recommended architecture for the new collections platform, covering front-end, back-end, data ingestion, and IIIF image serving  
      * Identification of third-party services and infrastructure requirements  
      * Technical risk assessment and any recommended pre-implementation investigations or prototypes

**Implementation**

* Back-end development  
* Front-end development  
* UX and design  
* CMS integration  
* QA and internal testing

Project management and consultancy will be provided to support the items above.

## 5\. Deliverables

**Discovery Deliverables**

* Presentation outlining long-term vision and “North Star”  
* Prioritized Phase 1 implementation backlog (“Must Haves”)  
* High-level Phase 2 roadmap with cost estimates  
* Summary documentation of discovery findings  
  * Summary document shall include a section dedicated to Phase 2 features. Each feature will be described on a single slide alongside an abstracted visualization. These materials will be used for fundraising purposes and should effectively communicate feature ideas to non-technical audiences.   
  * Summary document shall also include estimations for time and cost associated with features (either individual features or groups of features, based on the direction of FAMSF). 

**Implementation Deliverables**

* Automated TMS Collections data integration  
* Public search & discovery interface  
* Responsive object detail page implementation  
* Integration of the Online Collection with famsf.org CMS  
* IIIF image server to serve high-resolution zoomable images  
* Compliant with WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility standards

## 6\. Timetable 

The project can begin in late April 2026 and be completed by the end of December 2026, or as otherwise approved by the Fine Arts Museums of San Francisco.

Discovery shall not exceed 8 business weeks or $27,120 (16 days/120hrs)226) FAMSF and Copapp will partner to determine when Discovery may conclude. 

Implementation is expected to last approximately 16 weeks, followed by UAT support and a hardening phase.

## 7\. Charges, Expenses & Invoicing 

A maximum budget of **$350,000** will be made available to Cogapp for this project. Cogapp’s 2026 hourly rate is **$226**.

An indicative, estimated breakdown of that budget by phase follows:

* Project kick-off and FAMSF discovery  
  * Supporting FAMSF discovery activities  
  * Estimated cost \= $5,085  
* Discovery  
  * 8 weeks  
  * Using a team equivalent to 0.5 people full time  
  * Estimated cost \= $27,120  
* Implementation   
  * 8 sprints (16 weeks)  
  * Using a team equivalent to 2.5 people full time  
  * Estimated cost \= $271,200   
* PAUSE for UAT testing (\~6 weeks)  
  * Supporting FAMSF UAT activities  
  * Management time only  
  * Estimated cost \= $5,085  
* Hardening and public launch  
  * 2 sprints (4 weeks)  
  * Using a team equivalent to 1.25 people full time  
  * Estimated cost \= $33,900

**Total estimated cost of this plan= $342,390**

We recommend the remaining $7,610 is set aside to cover out of pocket expenses for travel, user testing costs and any third-party software tools. All expenses are recharged at cost, with no mark-up.

In practice, the total budget of $350,000 will be allocated to each phase in accordance with project needs and in consultation with FAMSF. 

Cogapp will not exceed the total budget without prior written agreement from FAMSF.

An invoice for work completed will be raised each month with an accompanying budget report.

Expenses are recharged with no markup.

## 8\. Critical Assumptions 

* TMS remains the system of record for collections data  
* FAMSF provides appropriate API/database access to TMS and image assets  
* FAMSF provides timely access to internal stakeholders for discovery  
* Discovery activities already conducted in-house are shared with Cogapp  
* Hosting and third-party licensing costs are excluded  
* Any new “Must Have” requirements introduced during implementation require reprioritisation or additional budget  
* UAT scheduling and feedback cycles are managed by FAMSF.
