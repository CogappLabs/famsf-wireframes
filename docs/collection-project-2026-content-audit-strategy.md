# Collection Project 2026

## Content Audit \+ Recommendation, April \- May 2026

**Owner:** Magnolia Molcan (Stephanie Amaro supporting)  
**Due date:** May 18, 2026

# **Context**

The 2026 Collections Project is a strategic initiative to modernize how FAMSF's digital collection is managed and experienced online. This content strategy work will be conducted to ensure that content requirements are incorporated into scoping for Phase 1 and that a clear content framework is in place to guide the Phase 2 vision.

---

# **Goal**

Audit how collection content is currently structured and presented, define key content recommendations to be incorporated into both Phases of the project.   
---

# **Scope**

This audit covers the collections section of the FAMSF website only. 

Audit areas:

* **Collection landing page and department/sub-pages**  
  * How is the collection introduced and navigated? What about the different collection areas? Is the hierarchy of information intuitive? What content should be updated at this time?  
* **Object detail pages**  
  * What content fields are currently displayed, which are empty or inconsistently populated, and what is missing entirely? Tagging-led discovery?  
* **Artfinder search and advanced filters**  
  * How are content/filters surfaced vs. buried by the current architecture? Nomenclature?   
  * What types of filters are missing?   
* **Editorial cross-linking**  
  * Where is cross-linking of content currently happening? Ex: Stories and audio content.   
* **Content debt**  
  * Is there any content that exists today on the website but is broken, orphaned, or inconsistently maintained? Ex: Old exhibition pages that we might want to link to from object details pages 

---

# **Questions this work will answer**

* Which fields are required on object detail pages for Phase 1, what's optional for Phase 1, what's aspirational for Phase 2?   
* How should collection narrative content (department introductions, thematic stories, educational context) be structured and incorporated into the new collections website?   
* How does content need to serve art explorers differently from researchers and educators? 

---

# **Deliverable**

* List of reviewed pages, whether content edits will be required or not  
* Audit of advanced filters and suggestions for updated filters ("ideal")  
* Content organization across key landings pages and Object detail pages  
  * Recommended content model for object detail pages: required fields, optional fields, display hierarchy  
  * Content recommendations for collection landing and department pages, scoped to Phase 1

Due: May 18, 2026

---

# **How this audit feeds into the larger project**

Content recommendations will inform front-end prioritization decisions and ensure content dependencies are understood before finalizing the decoupled architecture.

---

# **Not in scope**

* Main FAMSF ticketing website, shop, event pages, exhibition  
* Content creation or editorial work
